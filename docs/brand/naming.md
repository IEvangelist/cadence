# Naming

> **Status: recommendation for human decision.** The final name is a founder/legal
> call. This doc gives a shortlist, rationale, and a *sanity-level* availability
> check — **not** a cleared trademark opinion.

## Recommendation

**Keep `Cadence`.** It is already the codename, it is unusually *on-concept* for
this product, and it gives us the brand hook the whole kit is built around:

> A **cadence** is the chord progression that resolves a musical phrase — the
> moment it lands "home." Our AI helps you *build a phrase and land it.*
> **"Every idea, resolved."**

If legal wants clearer air in the software class (see the collision note below),
the strongest distinctive fallback is **`Cadenza`**.

## Shortlist

| Name | Meaning / why it fits | Vibe | Collision risk (sanity check) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cadence** ✅ *(recommend)* | The resolving progression — literally the product's promise. Easy to say/spell, one hook away from a tagline. | Confident, musical, timeless | **Elevated.** `Cadence Design Systems` (Nasdaq: CDNS) is a large software/EDA company; other "Cadence" apps exist (fitness, scheduling). Distinct product class + distinctive logo/wordmark help, but clear before public launch. |
| **Cadenza** | A virtuosic, often improvised solo flourish near a piece's end — evokes AI-assisted brilliance and expressiveness. | Expressive, a little romantic, premium | **Lower** than Cadence in software. Some music-hardware/education uses exist; verify. |
| **Motif** | A short musical idea that recurs and develops — perfect for an app that *generates and grows* ideas. | Modern, crisp, designerly | **Medium.** Common word; a defunct fintech "Motif" and various design uses. Broad but not obviously blocking in music software. |
| **Coda** | The concluding passage that brings a piece to a close. Short, punchy, ownable sound. | Minimal, sharp | **Medium–high.** `Coda` (now Grammarly) was a well-known doc app; the word is heavily used. |
| **Reprise** | To return/restate a theme — ties to continue/complete-a-piece and revision history. | Warm, literary | **Low–medium.** Music label "Reprise Records" exists; different class. |

## Domain / handle sanity check (verify at purchase time)

Exact-match `.com`s for common words are usually taken or premium, so plan on a
**qualified domain + consistent handle**:

- Prefer: `cadence.fm`, `cadence.audio`, `cadence.studio`, `getcadence.app`,
  `cadence.music`, or `trycadence.com`. Social handle target: `@cadenceapp` /
  `@cadence.studio`.
- `Cadenza` fallback: `cadenza.app`, `cadenza.fm`, `getcadenza.com`.
- These are **availability guesses**, not confirmations — check the registrar and
  the USPTO/EUIPO/relevant registries (and app-store name availability) before
  committing spend or a public launch.

## Naming system (once chosen)
- **Product:** Cadence.
- **Editions:** Cadence *Free* · Cadence *Pro* · Cadence *Studio* (maps to the
  freemium tiers in `docs/plan.md`).
- **Features:** plain-English, lower-case in prose — *the composer*, *stem split*,
  *the assistant*. Reserve the capital-C **Cadence** for the product itself.

## Members

Cadence runs as a **team of teams** (see `docs/plan.md` → "Delivery model — Squad" and
`docs/squad-ops.md`). The roster below has two bands: cross-cutting coordinator roles and
per-effort specialist squads. Ralph triages each `squad`-labelled issue to a specialist squad
using `.squad/routing.md`.
